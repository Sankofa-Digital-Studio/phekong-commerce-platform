# M2 Intern Guide

M2 delivers the visual-system reset and a safe booking foundation. Payments, account-credit redemption, refunds, inventory charging and transaction settlement remain in M3.

## Friendly summary

We are building the part that lets a customer choose an available treatment, briefly hold the slot, verify their contact details and receive confirmation without waiting for a staff member. Staff step in only when a special treatment or relevant care disclosure needs attention.

Think of the flow as:

`show availability → hold safely → verify customer → confirm → notify → handle exceptions`

The database decides whether a slot belongs to the customer. WhatsApp or SMS communicates that decision; messaging does not create the booking.

## Decisions you must preserve

- Guests can book without creating an account.
- Displayed availability is advisory until the database creates a hold.
- A valid booking confirms automatically after identity verification.
- Staff acknowledgement is not a routine confirmation gate.
- A replacement slot is secured before the original is released.
- Rescheduling uses exact server-time boundaries: 72 hours and 24 hours.
- WhatsApp is preferred; SMS is the reliability fallback.
- Health disclosures never drive marketing or product recommendations.
- Only Owner-assigned `care_reviewer` staff can view restricted care data.
- The Owner configures clinical-review treatments through a multi-select; the initial selection is empty.
- M2 must not pretend that payments, refunds or credits already exist.

## Intern communication template

> Hi team — M2 is booking-focused. Start from the linked issue and do not add payments or general marketing. Before coding, write down the state transition and RLS assumption you are changing. After coding, attach the commands run, test results, screenshots at 390px and 1440px, accessibility evidence, migration evidence where applicable, and the remaining risks. A green screenshot alone is not evidence: include the commit SHA and exact command output.

## How to report progress

Use this structure:

- **Issue and scope:** what acceptance criteria were addressed?
- **Commit SHA:** which exact code was tested?
- **Implementation:** what changed and why?
- **Verification:** exact commands and outcomes
- **Visual evidence:** relevant screen/state and viewport
- **Security/privacy evidence:** RLS, secret handling and sensitive-data checks
- **Known limitations:** what remains intentionally incomplete?
- **Next action:** who owns it?

## Design and Storybook expectations

- Reuse existing components before adding new ones.
- Consolidate route-level styling into shared tokens.
- Cover default, loading, empty, conflict, error, success, disabled, focus and reduced-motion states.
- Use the Basotho lens through rhythm, material, landscape and restraint; do not add generic cultural decoration.
- Validate mobile at 390px and desktop at 1440px.

## Anti-patterns

- Check availability and then insert without an atomic constraint.
- Release the original booking before securing the replacement.
- Make staff approval a hidden dependency.
- Put medical details in WhatsApp/SMS notifications.
- Use health disclosures for upselling.
- Treat notification delivery state as booking state.
- Add payment claims during M2.
- Report “tests pass” without command, result and commit SHA.
